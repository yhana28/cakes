Reminders

CAKE CARE INSTRUCTIONS

Carry from Bottom

Pickup and carry the cake box from the bottom only. Keep the cake level at all times.

Air-Conditioned Transport

Transport in an air-conditioned vehicle.

Keep Away from Heat

Keep away from sunlight, moisture, and heat.

Refrigerate

Store in refrigerator to maintain freshness.

Remove Before Serving

Remove from refrigerator 1 hour before serving.

Use Cake Stand

Use a sturdy cake stand when displaying.

Store Leftovers

Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator.

Consume Within 3 Days

For best taste and quality, consume the cake within 3 days.
